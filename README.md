# Aura-X: Multi-Domain AI Intelligence & Immersive Simulation Platform

## Overview
Aura-X is a **multi-agent intelligence and immersive simulation platform** developed by Manenzhe Technologies (MTech).

It combines:

- AI-driven decision intelligence  
- Real-time monitoring across systems  
- 2D/3D/4D immersive environments  

to solve real-world challenges across multiple sectors.

Aura-X enables users to:

- Experience natural and cultural environments through immersive tourism  
- Simulate industrial environments (mines, rail, infrastructure) for safety and training  
- Monitor and analyze real-world systems such as fleet, fuel, and infrastructure  
- Generate intelligent insights, alerts, and automated reports  

---

## Core Domains (Spheres)

Aura-X operates across multiple domains:

- **Tourism & Immersive Experiences (Origin Module)**  
  Virtual exploration of cultural, natural, and heritage sites  

- **Fleet & Fuel Intelligence**  
  Monitoring vehicle activity, fuel usage, and driver behavior  

- **Municipal Systems**  
  Service delivery monitoring, infrastructure, and asset management  

- **Mining & Industrial Safety**  
  Equipment monitoring, worker safety, and predictive risk detection  

- **Rail & Transport Systems**  
  Operational monitoring, efficiency, and safety analytics  

---

## Key Features

- **Multi-Agent Architecture**  
  Reasoning, Monitoring, Recommendation, and Response agents  

- **Decision Intelligence Engine**  
  Detects anomalies, predicts risks, and recommends actions  

- **Immersive Simulation (2D/3D/4D)**  
  Multi-sensory environments including visual, audio, and haptic feedback  

- **Provider-Agnostic Integration**  
  Works with any tracking, fuel, IoT, or maintenance system  

- **IoT & Sensor Integration**  
  Real-time data ingestion and monitoring  

- **Dashboard Studio**  
  Pre-built dashboards for live operational visibility  

- **Smart Reporting Engine**  
  Automated PDF reports and email notifications  

- **Cloud Integration Ready**  
  Scalable deployment (Huawei Cloud adapter – extendable to other providers)  

- **Expandable Architecture**  
  Future AR/VR support and multi-user collaboration
